# Provenance

repository from *Intrinsic Quantum Geometric Tensor on the Grassmannian and Spectral Lower Bounds
on Dissipation* (the June 2026 LaTeX edition; Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20768258): Lemma 3.1,
Corollary 3.1, Theorem 3.2, Theorem 6.1, Corollary 6.1 and the two-level analysis of Sec. 6.
